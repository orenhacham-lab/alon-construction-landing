## Platform

web

## Users
- Main site (`/`): building owners, house committees (ועדי בתים) and public bodies who need structural restoration, reinforcement or dangerous-building treatment.
- Finishing-works page (`/avodot-gmar`): private homeowners in central Israel who are building or renovating a house or apartment and need finishing work (gypsum, flooring, woodwork) done to a high standard without juggling several contractors.

## Product Purpose
Lead-generation landing pages for אלון עבודות בניה בע"מ. Success is a qualified lead: a WhatsApp message, a phone call, or a submitted form that becomes an on-site meeting and measurement.

## Positioning
One contractor for every stage: the company runs structure, development, gypsum, flooring, woodwork and finishing itself. One address, one schedule, one quote, one party accountable for the result, so nothing falls between trades.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive mostly from Google Ads on mobile. The process: on-site meeting and measurement, detailed itemized quote with materials and a committed schedule, execution with a building technician (הנדסאי בניין) and work manager on site giving updates, joint walkthrough of every detail before handover.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: gypsum (dropped ceilings, hidden lighting, niches, TV units, acoustic ceilings, decorative work); flooring (ceramic, parquet, stone, marble, polished concrete without tiles, bathroom tiling and cladding); woodwork (pergolas, decks, fences, canopies); structure, renovation and building rehabilitation.
- Finishing levels from basic to luxury, always to Israeli standard.
- Service area: Modiin, Shoham, Lod, Ramla, Rosh HaAyin, Elad, Yehud, Or Yehuda, Kiryat Ono, Petah Tikva, Be'er Ya'akov, Rishon LeZion, Ness Ziona, Rehovot and surroundings. Office in Moshav Hadid.
- Leads go to `/api/lead` (Resend email) and fire GTM `form_submit_success` with Enhanced Conversions user data.
- Next.js 14 App Router, Tailwind, RTL Hebrew, hosted on Hostinger.
